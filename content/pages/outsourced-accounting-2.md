## Your accounting department without the overhead

Running a real estate portfolio, a professional service business, a video production company, or a retail operation means wearing a dozen hats, and sooner or later "chief financial officer" becomes one you can't wear well. Outsourced accounting gives you a full department: bookkeeping, controller-level review, and CPA oversight, without the cost of hiring staff to do it. Abramson & Company has run this exact model since founding the firm in 2005, moving Westport and Fairfield County businesses off spreadsheets and onto systems that actually show how the business is doing.

Most firms selling "outsourced accounting" are really renting out a bookkeeper. Ours is different. Every account gets CPA-level review, so the numbers you see are accurate enough to make real decisions on, not just accurate enough to file a return. Whether you're tracking property draws, project retainers, production budgets, or seasonal inventory swings, that's the difference between a data-entry service and an accounting department built around how your business actually works.

## Bookkeeping

Good bookkeeping is the foundation of every other financial decision you make. When your books are clean and current, you can see what is happening in your business without waiting for a tax deadline to find out. When they aren't, small errors pile up and turn into stressful clean-up projects.

Our bookkeeping covers the daily work that keeps your records dependable. We classify your transactions so income and expenses land in the right accounts, and we track expenses so you always know where your money is going. We also handle bill payment and invoicing, which means vendors get paid on time and your clients receive accurate invoices without you chasing either one.

Every month, you receive clear reporting that is easy to read, even if accounting is not your favorite subject. Because our team works inside a secure online platform, you can share documents and check in on your numbers in real time instead of trading emails back and forth.

Bookkeeping is included in our bundled outsourced accounting services for a fixed monthly fee, so you know what you are paying and what you are getting. Your work is reviewed by a CPA, and several members of our team know your account well enough to step in for one another, so nothing stalls when someone is out.

Whether you manage rental properties, run a retail shop, or juggle client projects, we set up your books to match how your business really operates. The result is less time on paperwork and more confidence in the numbers.

## Payroll

Payroll is one of those tasks that seems simple until a filing deadline is missed or a paycheck is wrong. Handling it well takes accuracy, timing, and attention to changing rules, and it is not where most business owners want to spend their week.

Our payroll service takes it off your plate. We process payroll on your schedule and handle the payroll tax filings, so your obligations are met on time. At year-end, we prepare W-2 and 1099 forms for your employees and contractors, which removes a major source of January stress.

Your team benefits too. Employees and employers get electronic access to pay information, so pay stubs and records are easy to find without a call to the office. If your business offers retirement benefits or has wage garnishments to administer, we take care of pension and garnishment remittance so those payments go out correctly.

Because payroll connects directly to your books and your taxes, handling it alongside your accounting means the numbers agree from one report to the next. There is no re-entering data or reconciling between separate vendors. A CPA-led team looks at the whole picture, so payroll costs show up accurately in your financial statements and your tax planning.

For Westport and Fairfield County employers, including small businesses, nonprofits, and growing service firms, this means fewer surprises and a payroll process you can stop worrying about. You stay focused on your people and your clients while we keep the details in order.
