## Accounting built for real estate professionals

Agents chasing commissions, brokers running teams, investors with five or six properties, developers mid-construction, property managers reconciling rent rolls: real estate income never looks the same twice. Generic advice tells you depreciation exists. It doesn't tell you how to apply it to your triplex in Westport or your flip in New Canaan.

Abramson & Company has worked with real estate clients across Westport, CT, Fairfield County, and the tri-state area since 2005. We build the financial side of your business around how you earn money, and we answer the phone when you call.

## Tax strategy built around your calendar, not April's

Agents face commission income that swings month to month, with quarterly estimates due whether the deal closed or not. Investors face depreciation and passive loss questions that need answers before year-end.

- Depreciation schedules and entity structure reviewed before year-end
- Cost segregation flagged on new acquisitions
- Quarterly estimates based on what you're earning now
- Multi-property portfolios reviewed as a whole, so losses aren't wasted

The goal: fewer surprises at filing time.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Real estate accounting

**Q: Do you work with real estate investors outside of Connecticut?**
A: Yes. Alongside clients in Westport, Fairfield County, and the tri-state area of CT, NY, and NJ, we also serve real estate investors and professionals in California, particularly the Bay Area. Our secure online platform supports real-time collaboration regardless of location or time zone, with the same direct access to your team.

**Q: Can you help with 1031 exchange tax planning?**
A: Yes. Our Tax Plus service includes proactive review of 1031 exchange timelines, depreciation schedules, and cost segregation opportunities before a transaction closes, so the tax strategy is built into the deal rather than addressed after the fact.

**Q: How does pricing work for real estate accounting services?**
A: Bookkeeping, tax, payroll, and advisory support are bundled under a fixed monthly fee. You know your cost upfront and can add services as your property portfolio grows, without an unpredictable invoice at year-end.

**Q: What real estate professionals do you typically work with?**
A: We work with agents, brokers, investors, developers, and property managers handling rental income, commission income, multi-property entity structures, and construction-related accounting throughout Westport, Fairfield County, and the tri-state area. We have served real estate clients since 2005.

**Q: How do you handle depreciation and cost segregation?**
A: Depreciation gets tricky when you weigh a cost segregation study against its own cost, or decide whether a rental loss is deductible under the passive activity rules. We review depreciation schedules and entity structure before year-end and flag when a study makes sense on a new acquisition.

**Q: I'm an agent with uneven commissions. How do you handle quarterly estimates?**
A: Quarterly estimates are due whether or not a deal closed. We set them to reflect what you're actually earning rather than last year's numbers, and adjust as your commission income swings, so you avoid both overpaying early and a surprise bill later.

**Q: Can you look at my whole property portfolio instead of one property at a time?**
A: Yes. For clients holding several properties, we review the portfolio as a whole so losses on one property aren't quietly wasted while another owes tax it didn't need to. Business Foundation Services also reviews how each property is held within your entity structure.

**Q: Can my lender or partner see my financials?**
A: Yes. Our secure online platform lets you share financials with lenders, partners, or attorneys when a deal needs proof of numbers. You can see income and expenses by property, pull reports for a refinance, sale, or due diligence packet, and message your team directly inside it.

**Q: What happens if my main contact is out of the office?**
A: Our team-oriented culture means multiple team members know each client well enough to step in for one another. Whether your question is about a rental, a 1031 deadline, or your entity structure, someone familiar with your situation can help without you starting over.
