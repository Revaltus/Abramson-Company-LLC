- **Not sure whether to form an LLC, S-corp, or C-corp?** We run an entity type analysis against your tax picture, how you plan to pay yourself, and your long-term goals.

- **Unsure which retirement plan makes sense?** We run a retirement plan analysis so the plan fits your structure and income from the start.

- **Lenders or partners will check your numbers?** We review your business plan so the figures hold up.
- **Nobody to call at 10 p.m.?** You get a direct line to a CPA, not a call center.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Business foundation services

**Q: What are business foundation services?**
A: Business foundation services are CPA-guided support for launching a business correctly the first time. They cover entity type analysis, retirement plan analysis, business plan review, registration and licensing guidance, and accounting system setup, so new owners in Westport, CT make decisions with a full picture instead of guesswork.

**Q: How do you decide between an LLC and an S-corp?**
A: We model your expected income, self-employment tax exposure, and long-term plans, whether that's bringing on partners or eventually selling. There's no single right answer. A retail shop, a real estate investor, an attorney with a new practice, and a video production company with similar revenue can land in different structures based on how they operate and grow.

**Q: What does entity analysis cover before I file anything?**
A: We weigh LLC, S-corp, C-corp, and other structures against your tax picture, how you plan to pay yourself, and your long-term goals. That includes self-employment tax exposure, distribution rules, and filing obligations, so the structure supports where the business is headed rather than a generic template.

**Q: Do you help with retirement plan analysis for new business owners?**
A: Yes. Once your entity structure and compensation approach are in place, we help you identify the retirement plan, such as a SEP-IRA, Solo 401(k), or SIMPLE IRA, that fits your business and income. Starting early means the plan works with your tax strategy from day one.

**Q: Do you help with business registration in Connecticut?**
A: Yes. We guide new business owners through registration and licensing requirements in Connecticut, New York, and New Jersey, depending on where the business operates, so the paperwork matches the entity structure and tax setup already in place.

**Q: Can you review my business plan?**
A: Yes. We review your numbers so they hold up under real scrutiny before you present them to a lender or partner. It also helps confirm that the entity choice and accounting setup support the plan you are describing.

**Q: Can I add payroll or bookkeeping later?**
A: Yes. Business foundation services are the starting point. As your business grows, you can add bookkeeping, payroll, and tax under our bundled fixed monthly fee, with everything visible in real time on one secure online platform.

**Q: Do you work with video production companies and professional service providers?**
A: Yes. We have direct experience with the entity and accounting questions that come up for video production companies, consultants, attorneys, real estate professionals, and retail owners, where the structure you choose at the start affects how you bill, pay yourself, and grow.
