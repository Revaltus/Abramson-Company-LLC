## Accounting built around how video production companies work

Video production income rarely lands the same way twice. One month you invoice a six-figure commercial shoot. The next, you wait forty-five days on a payment while covering gear rentals, freelance editors, and a crew of contractors.

Abramson & Company works with production companies in Westport, CT and Fairfield County. Our bookkeeping, payroll, and tax services are built around project-based revenue and equipment-heavy budgets, so you get fewer surprises at tax time and a clearer read on cash flow between projects.

Our [bookkeeping](/services/bookkeeping) and [outsourced accounting](/services/outsourced-accounting) services are bundled under one fixed monthly fee. You see real-time numbers on a secure online platform, not a monthly PDF.

- Project-by-project budget tracking, so you know margin on each shoot
- Client invoicing and collections support to steady cash flow between productions
- Vendor and equipment rental payment tracking
- Monthly reconciliation and reporting you can read without decoding spreadsheets

## Payroll for crews, freelancers, and contractors

One shoot day can involve a salaried production manager, three freelance camera operators paid on a 1099, and a per-project gaffer. Classifying each person correctly is where production companies often run into trouble.

Our [payroll](/services/payroll) service handles W-2 staff, 1099 contractors, and per-project crew in one system. We flag misclassification risk early and issue year-end W-2 and 1099 forms.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Video Production

**Q: Do you work with video production companies outside Westport, CT?**
A: Yes. Abramson & Company serves production companies throughout Fairfield County, the tri-state area of Connecticut, New York, and New Jersey, and clients in California, especially the Bay Area. Everything is supported through a secure online platform for real-time collaboration.

**Q: How do you handle payroll for a mix of W-2 staff and freelance crew?**
A: Our payroll service runs W-2 employees, 1099 freelancers, and per-project crew through one system instead of three processes. We track worker classification, filing deadlines, and year-end W-2 and 1099 forms, flag misclassification risk early, and keep a payroll record that holds up if a filing is questioned.

**Q: Can you help with Connecticut's digital media tax credit?**
A: Yes. We help production companies determine whether their activity and expenses qualify for Connecticut's sliding-scale credit for digital media and motion picture production, and we walk through the documentation the state requires as part of broader tax planning for media businesses.

**Q: What's included in your bundled monthly fee for production companies?**
A: The bundle typically includes bookkeeping, outsourced accounting, payroll, and tax services under one fixed monthly fee, accessed through a secure online platform. Exact scope depends on crew size and project volume, which we review together during a consultation.

**Q: Do you offer support if my production company is just starting out?**
A: Yes. Business Foundation Services help new production owners choose the right entity structure, such as staying a sole proprietor or moving to an S-corp, and set up financial systems from the start, so the move from freelance work to a full company happens on solid footing.

**Q: How does equipment depreciation work for cameras and lighting?**
A: Cameras, lenses, lighting rigs, and edit bay hardware depreciate on a schedule that rewards planning purchases rather than just recording them afterward. We help you time gear buys against Section 179 and bonus depreciation rules so a large purchase works in your favor at tax time.

**Q: Can I deduct a home studio or home office?**
A: Often, yes. Many production owners run pre-production, editing, or client meetings from a home office or converted studio. We calculate the deduction correctly, including square footage, utilities, and depreciation, so you claim what you're entitled to without raising a flag.

**Q: Which production expenses can be deducted?**
A: Location fees, travel to shoots, insurance, and post-production software subscriptions all carry deduction rules specific to project-based creative work. We track them against your bookkeeping records throughout the year so nothing is missed at filing time.

**Q: What do Business Management Services cover for a growing production house?**
A: They cover the longer view of owning a growing creative business: profit distribution, retirement planning, and the financial decisions that come with a full crew, a studio lease, and multiple concurrent projects. They pair with Business Foundation Services, which handle entity choice and system setup.
