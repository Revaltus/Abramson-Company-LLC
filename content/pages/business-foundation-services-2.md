## Entity type analysis: choosing the right structure

Choosing between an LLC, S-corp, C-corp, or another structure is one of the first big decisions you'll make, and it affects your taxes, your paperwork, and how you take money out of the business. Our entity type analysis looks at your whole picture: what you expect to earn, how you plan to pay yourself, whether you have partners or investors, and where you want the business to go over the long term.

We walk through each option in plain language, including what it would likely mean for your tax bill and what ongoing requirements come with it. A real estate investor holding rental properties has different needs than a consultant launching a solo practice or a retail owner opening a first storefront, so we don't hand out one-size-fits-all answers.

You'll leave the conversation knowing which structure we recommend, why we recommend it, and what to do next. Because the same CPA-led team that helps you choose your entity can also handle your bookkeeping, payroll, and tax work, the decision is made with the full picture in mind. And if your business grows or changes, we'll revisit the choice with you, so your structure keeps working as hard as you do.

## Accounting system setup built around your business

Good books start with a good setup. Many new owners begin with a generic template or a spreadsheet and end up untangling it at tax time. Our accounting system setup gives you a bookkeeping structure designed around how your business actually earns and spends money.

We start by understanding your business. A video production company tracking project-based revenue needs something different from a retail shop managing inventory or a professional practice billing by the hour. From there, we build a chart of accounts, connect the tools you'll use day to day, and set up simple routines for classifying transactions, tracking expenses, paying bills, and invoicing customers.

You'll also get access to our secure online platform, so you and your CPA can work from the same information in real time. No more wondering whether your numbers are current or whether something was missed.

If you'd like ongoing help once you're up and running, our bookkeeping, payroll, and outsourced accounting services can be bundled for a fixed monthly fee. Either way, you start with clean, organized records and the confidence that comes from knowing where your business stands.

## Retirement plan analysis from day one

It's easy to put retirement planning off when you're focused on launching, but the plan you choose early can shape your tax savings for years. Our retirement plan analysis helps you find the option that fits your business structure and income from the start, whether that's a SEP-IRA, a Solo 401(k), a SIMPLE IRA, or another plan.

Each plan comes with its own contribution limits, administrative requirements, and rules about employees. The right answer depends on whether you're a solo owner or plan to hire, how steady your income is, and how much you want to set aside. We explain the tradeoffs clearly, so you can make a choice you feel good about rather than guessing.

Because retirement planning connects to your entity choice and your tax strategy, we look at all three together. That means contributions are timed and structured to work with your overall tax plan, not against it.

As your business changes, we'll check back in. A plan that made sense in year one may need a second look as your income grows or your team expands, and we're glad to walk through it with you.
